Moving inventory checklist

"It's in one of the kitchen boxes" stops being helpful at box 40. A simple inventory, made while you pack, means you can find the kettle on the first night, prove to the movers what went missing, and unpack in a sensible order.

Before you pack

  • Declutter first. Everything you don't move is a box you don't pack, label, carry and unpack. Sell or donate as you go.
  • Pick a numbering system. Number every box (1, 2, 3…) and write the destination room on it. The number links the box to your list.
  • Photograph valuables and electronics before they go in a box, including how cables are plugged in.

While you pack: one line per box

  • Box number and destination room
  • What's inside, specific enough to search ("bread knife", not "kitchen stuff")
  • Fragile? Heavy? Open first?
  • A photo of the open box before you tape it

Label two sides and the top. Boxes get stacked, and the label you wrote is always the one facing the wall.

The "open first" box

Pack one box per person with the first night and morning: chargers, toiletries, medication, a change of clothes, bedding, toilet paper, a kettle, mugs, coffee or tea, basic tools, scissors, light bulbs and paperwork for the new place. Number it 1.

With movers

  • Count boxes as they leave and as they arrive, against your list.
  • Note damage on the delivery paperwork before you sign it.
  • Your photos and box contents are your evidence if you need to claim for loss or damage.

After the move

Unpack by room, kitchen and bedrooms first. Keep the list: things that stay boxed in a garage or basement are exactly the ones you'll be looking for in six months.

Do it with your phone instead of a spreadsheet
