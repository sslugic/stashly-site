Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026.

What we store

If you sign in, Stashly stores your email address and your inventory: item and storage photos, names, descriptions, price estimates and locations. This lets us back up your data and sync it between your devices. If you never sign in, your inventory stays on your device.

How the AI works

When you scan a photo or ask a question, the photo or text is sent to our server and processed by Google's Gemini AI to identify items and answer you. We don't use your photos for advertising.

Purchases

Scan packs and subscriptions are paid through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details.

Sharing

When you share an item or storage spot, anyone with that link can see that page and its photo until the link expires. Nothing else in your inventory is shared.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: Settings, then Erase, removes your inventory from your device and from your synced account.
  • Full account and photo deletion, or questions: email slugics@gmail.com.
