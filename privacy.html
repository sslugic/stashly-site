Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

What we store

If you sign in, Stashly stores your email address (or the private relay address Apple gives us), your name if you share it, and your inventory: item and storage photos, names, descriptions, price estimates and where things are kept. This lets us back up your data and sync it between your devices. If you never sign in, your inventory stays on your device.

How the AI works

When you scan a photo, make an AI product photo or ask a question, the photo or text is sent to our server and processed by Google's Gemini AI to identify items and answer you. Google doesn't use it to train its models under our paid API terms. We don't use your photos for advertising.

Marketplace, messages and location

If you list something on the Marketplace, the listing (photos, title, price and an approximate area) is visible to other Stashly users. Messages you exchange with buyers or sellers are stored so both sides can read them. To show listings near you, Stashly asks for your approximate location while you use the Marketplace; your exact location is never shown to anyone. Reports you file and users you block are stored to keep the Marketplace safe.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device token so we can tell you about new messages and activity on your listings.

Purchases

Stashly Plus and credit top-ups are paid through the App Store or Google Play, and processed for us by RevenueCat. We see what you bought and when, never your card details.

Sharing

When you share an item or storage spot, anyone with that link can see that page and its photo until the link expires. When you invite someone to a place, they can see and edit that place's inventory. Nothing else is shared.

No tracking

Stashly doesn't track you across other apps or websites, doesn't show ads and doesn't sell your data.

Website analytics

This website (not the app) counts page visits with PostHog so we know which pages are read and which links bring people here, such as campaign tags in a link (utm_source, utm_campaign). It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, records no clicks or what you type, and drops everything else from the page address. PostHog receives your IP address to make the request, and we don't use it to identify you. If your browser sends Do Not Track, nothing is counted.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings, then Delete my account, permanently deletes your account, inventory, photos, places, listings, messages and credits.
  • Erase your inventory only: Settings, then Erase all items & storage.
